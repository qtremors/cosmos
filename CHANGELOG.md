# Cosmos Changelog

> **Project:** Cosmos
>
> **Version:** 0.1.1
>
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-20

---

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-20

- Collapse and expand telemetry, jet controls, object lists, settings, information, and performance panels individually.
- Pilot a 20-metre jet with thrust, momentum, braking, smooth banked turns, engine plumes, and a separate chase camera.
- Start camera and jet travel at 100 km/s, with slower flight near surfaces.
- Keep flight shortcuts responsive after clicking panels.
- Tap or hold Shift to accumulate travel speed, keep it on release, and use S to return to normal cruise.
- Show live nearest-object distances in all telemetry views, measured from the ship while piloting.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-01-06

- Remember graphics, lighting, visibility, time, and information-panel preferences in the browser.
- Restore the black-hole disk at Low quality and keep scene labels behind interface panels.
- Exclude hidden fictional destinations from keyboard navigation and clean up failed scene startup.
- Fix the local performance-preview launcher on Windows.
- Consolidate setup guidance, physical-model limits, and the remaining device-validation backlog.

## [0.0.9] - 2025-12-31

- Add astronomical reference checks, runtime and input regressions, browser test scenarios, and local profiling tools.

## [0.0.8] - 2025-12-28

- Add searchable object navigation, keyboard and touch controls, responsive panels, date and quality settings, and performance readouts.

## [0.0.7] - 2025-12-27

- Integrate the scene controller, adaptive flight and tracking, independent lighting, automatic exposure, and analytic solar eclipses.

## [0.0.6] - 2025-12-26

- Add Arishem and his interior models with dedicated lighting, Explorer, The Kyln, and a steady distant Sun marker.

## [0.0.5] - 2025-12-25

- Add Quantumania terrain, structures, ships, and inhabitants with deferred loading, retries, and model eviction.

## [0.0.4] - 2025-12-20

- Add Alien X and the black hole beyond the Solar region, preserve their relative placement, and introduce system navigation boundaries.

## [0.0.3] - 2025-12-17

- Add the remaining planets, major moons, rings, dwarf planets, Halley, orbit guides, and visible asteroid and Kuiper belts.

## [0.0.2] - 2025-12-13

- Render the Sun, Earth, and Moon with surface textures, atmospheric glow, dated orientation, and selectable close-ups.

## [0.0.1] - 2025-12-10

- Establish the React and Three.js viewer, common physical scale, dated orbital calculations, and owned scene resources.
